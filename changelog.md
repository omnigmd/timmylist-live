# v2.0.0
- Renamed to Timmy Tracker, with a new logo made from the Timmylist emblem.
- Timmylist pop-up from the main menu: link status, completed levels, points and every level with your best %.
- Level page badge with rank, points and your best, plus a button that opens the level on timmylist.org.
- In-level display with position and opacity settings.
- Catch-up sync of progress you already had, on launch and on demand.

- Completion notice with points, optional new-best notice.
- Settings for every feature.
